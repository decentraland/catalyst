# Content server configuration

The content server can be configured by environment variables.

## Env vars (WIP)

- `DENYLIST_URLS`: List of denylists used by the content server. Separated by new lines `\n`. Default: "https://config.decentraland.org/denylist"
- `DENYLIST_FILE_NAME`: Filename to be used as local denylist. Default: "denylist.txt"
- `SYNC_IGNORED_ENTITY_TYPES`: Ignored entity types, separated by comma. Prevents certain entities from being pulled from DAO catalysts. Default: ""
- `PENDING_DEPLOYMENT_TTL`: Fixed lifetime of a partial (multi-request) deployment, in milliseconds, counted from the arrival of its first request (before its body is read). Retries do not extend it, and a batch that is still being processed when it ends is rejected before its files are stored or the entity is published. Default: "3600000" (1h)
- `MAX_PENDING_DEPLOYMENTS_PER_DEPLOYER`: Max pending uploads per deployer, including expired uploads awaiting cleanup. Excess uploads get a `429` whose `Retry-After` is the next cleanup run (see `PENDING_DEPLOYMENTS_CLEANUP_INTERVAL`) once the oldest upload holding the quota has expired, since expired uploads stay charged until reclaimed; at least 1 s. Default: "10"
- `MAX_PENDING_BYTES_PER_DEPLOYER`: Max staged/reserved bytes per deployer. Expired uploads stay charged until their content is deleted. Excess batches get a `429` with `Retry-After`, as for the upload count; an upload that alone needs more than this or `MAX_PENDING_BYTES` gets a `400`. Default: "1073741824" (1 GiB)
- `MAX_PENDING_BYTES`: Max staged/reserved bytes across the server. Excess batches get a `429` with `Retry-After`, as for the upload count, over every deployer's uploads. Default: "53687091200" (50 GiB)
- `MAX_PARTIAL_UPLOAD_BYTES_PER_MINUTE`: Accepted partial batch bytes per deployer per fixed one-minute window, retries included. Excess batches get a `429` whose `Retry-After` is the time left in the window (at least 1 s); a single batch larger than this gets a `400`, still counted. Must be at least `MAX_UPLOAD_FILE_SIZE` plus 10 MiB (the entity file cap), since a file can't be split across batches. Default: "536870912" (512 MiB)
- `CONTENT_LOCK_CONNECTIONS`: Connections of the dedicated pool holding the advisory lock shared by deployments and excluded by garbage collection, in addition to `PG_POOL_SIZE`. Default: "16"
- `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_UPLOAD_BYTES`: Max POST /entities body bytes buffered in memory at once across all clients; excess uploads get a `503`. Partial batches are exempt from the per-IP daily quota (not the per-minute burst limit) and bounded by this, the per-source share below and their account's byte quotas instead. Each request reserves at least `MIN_UPLOAD_RESERVATION_BYTES`. Must fit one maximum-size request's peak: `MAX_UPLOAD_TOTAL_SIZE` plus a copy of one file (up to `MAX_UPLOAD_FILE_SIZE`). Default: that peak or "4294967296" (4 GiB), whichever is larger
- `MIN_UPLOAD_RESERVATION_BYTES`: Least each POST /entities request reserves from `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_UPLOAD_BYTES`, however small its declared body, covering its per-request overhead (form fields, parser buffers, the socket); a larger request reserves its own peak. It bounds concurrent uploads at `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_UPLOAD_BYTES` / `MIN_UPLOAD_RESERVATION_BYTES` (256 by default); excess uploads get a `503`. Must be at most `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_UPLOAD_BYTES`. Default: "16777216" (16 MiB)
- `TRUSTED_CLIENT_IP_HEADER`: Header holding the client address that the POST /entities rate limits and the per-source upload limits key on. Catalyst runs behind catalyst-owner's nginx, which forwards the connecting address in `X-Real-IP` (`proxy_set_header X-Real-IP $remote_addr` in `local/nginx/include/content_proxy.conf`); keyed on the socket address instead, every client would share nginx's address and so one budget. A request without the header is keyed on its socket address. Set it to the header another proxy writes, or to an empty string only when the process is reached directly (clients could otherwise pick their own address); empty logs a startup warning. Default: "x-real-ip"
- `MAX_CONCURRENT_UPLOADS_PER_SOURCE`: Max POST /entities bodies in flight at once from one client source (the `TRUSTED_CLIENT_IP_HEADER` address, else the socket address), checked before the body is read and held until the request ends; excess uploads get a `429` with `Retry-After`. Applies to regular deployments and partial batches alike. Default: "4"
- `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_UPLOAD_BYTES_PER_SOURCE`: Max declared POST /entities body bytes in flight at once from one client source; a body without a `Content-Length` counts as `MAX_UPLOAD_TOTAL_SIZE`. Excess uploads get a `429` with `Retry-After`. Must be at least `MAX_UPLOAD_TOTAL_SIZE`. Default: `MAX_UPLOAD_TOTAL_SIZE`
- `MAX_UPLOAD_FIELD_COUNT`: Max non-file form fields in a POST /entities request; more get a `413` naming the limit. The default fits `entityId`, `partial` and the three `authChain[i][type|payload|signature]` fields of the longest accepted auth chain (10 links). Default: "32"
- `MAX_UPLOAD_FIELD_SIZE`: Max bytes of one non-file form field value in a POST /entities request; a larger value gets a `413` naming the limit. Legitimate values are at most a few KiB (an EIP-1654 signature), so with the field count a request holds at most 1 MiB of fields in memory. Default: "32768" (32 KiB)
- `MULTIPART_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS`: Max time to receive a POST /entities body; a slower upload is aborted with a `408` and its upload slot released. Default: "300000" (5 min)
- `PENDING_DEPLOYMENTS_CLEANUP_INTERVAL`: How often the job that reclaims expired pending deployments (their unreferenced staged content, then their accounting) runs, in milliseconds. Expired uploads stay charged against quotas until it reclaims them. Default: "300000" (5 min)
- `GARBAGE_COLLECTION_INTERVAL`: How often the incremental garbage collection sweep runs when `GARBAGE_COLLECTION` is enabled, in milliseconds. Default: "3600000" (1h)
